1. Study design

Two independent bulk RNA-seq datasets profile the same three EGFR-mutant NSCLC cell lines (PC9, H1975, HCC827) under osimertinib resistance, giving 9 sensitive vs 9 resistant per dataset (36 samples total).

Role Accession Lines Samples Contrast Gene space
Discovery GSE202859 PC9, H1975, HCC827 9 vs 9 Parental vs -OR NCBI raw counts, Entrez→symbol
Validation GSE193258 PC9, H1975, HCC827 9 vs 9 DMSO vs osi_DTP 3w NCBI raw counts, Entrez→symbol

Both matrices use NCBI’s uniformly reprocessed counts (GRCh38.p13), so the two datasets share a gene universe and the comparison is alignment-harmonised by construction.

Label policy: sensitive = parental / DMSO; resistant = -OR / osimertinib DTP 3 weeks. Acute 24 h exposure, short/long washout and shRNA/perturbation samples are excluded.

3c. Sensitivity — biological reversibility (D7)

The validation cohort carries an excluded time-course arm: osi_acute (24 h), short_washout, and long_washout (7 d / 72 h / 96 h) samples. The locked discovery models (persisted per D9) are applied to these samples after a blind VST refit on all 60 samples (scripts/02b_vst_extend.R — the primary matrix is untouched). No accuracy is claimed; the question is whether P(Resistant) drifts back toward the parental state after drug withdrawal.

7. Leakage controls

- Cohorts kept separate; no pooled cross-validation. 
- Splits group by cell line; all replicates of a line stay in one fold. 
- Feature selection, scaling and tuning run inside each training fold. 
- External validation cohort evaluated once, after the pipeline is locked. 
- Full model × K grid reported; bootstrap CIs included.

8. Limitations

  • Only 3 cell lines; leave-one-line-out CV has 3 folds — results are exploratory.
  • The resistant arm is drug-treated while the sensitive arm is vehicle, so the signature is partly entangled with acute drug exposure (a confound inherent to both datasets).
  • -OR (stable) and osi_DTP (reversible) are different resistance states; accepted per policy.
  • Predictive genes are candidate biomarkers, not causal drivers.
